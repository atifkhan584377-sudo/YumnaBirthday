A SMALL LETTER
For You, Yumna ♡

I could have just sent you a message saying “Happy Birthday” and called it a day.

But somehow that felt way too ordinary for someone who deserves at least a little bit of extraordinary.

So I made this tiny world instead. It has quests, suspicious files, unnecessary dramatic effects, memories, and probably more effort than was strictly necessary.

And honestly... that's kind of the point.

Some people become important through huge moments. Other times it happens through tiny things — random conversations, stupid jokes, unexpected memories, and moments you don't realize you'll remember until much later.

I hope this next year gives you plenty of those moments. The funny ones. The completely random ones. The beautiful ones. Even the slightly embarrassing ones.

Because those are the things that eventually become a story.

And this little website isn't meant to be the end of that story.

It's supposed to be the beginning of a place where the story can keep collecting memories.

So today... enjoy your birthday. Be a menace if necessary. Accept the compliments. And please pretend all this effort was completely normal.

— from someone who definitely didn't overthink this ♡
